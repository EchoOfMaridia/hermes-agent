FOR AVAHI The Drive-Thru panel is the most diagnostic of the three because every failure mode has a unique label (REFUSE / ORDER / HOLD / REJECT / CONFUSED). General Concepts says refusals are −4× penalty; the badge here should sort to surface refusals at the top. "HOLD" is the correct answer for cases like #67 — the agent should ask for clarification, not refuse. Cases where GPT-5.6 and GLM 4.7 both refuse but MiniMax / Gemma / gpt-oss hold are the most interesting — they're the cases where a stock frontier model falls into the simplest failure mode.
