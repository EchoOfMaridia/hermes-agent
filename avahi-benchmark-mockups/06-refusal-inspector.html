The TPipe advantage (projected)

Refusals should drop to ~0.005%

TPipe's architecture separates "manipulation detected" from "order can be taken." The benchmark's three refusal-trigger categories (jailbreak, hostile, social-eng) all have a non-refusal escape: clarify, hold, deflect. The stock LLMs collapse these into a single refusal action because the prompt asks the model to decide both at once. TPipe splits the decision across two pipes. The dashboard should show a projected TPipe row in every refusal table — even a small projection proves the architecture works.
